# Can I pick up my order from a branch?

Yes. Choose **Store Pickup** at checkout and select a branch. Pickup is free. You will get an SMS when the order is ready, usually within **24 hours** in Cairo and Giza branches. Bring your ID and the order number. Orders not collected within **7 days** are cancelled and refunded.
