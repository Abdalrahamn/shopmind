# What is the difference between the 14-day and 30-day return windows?

If the product is **unopened**, you have **30 days** to return it. If you **opened** it, you have **14 days**, and it must be in perfect condition with all accessories. Defective products can always be returned within 30 days. Clearance items are final sale.
