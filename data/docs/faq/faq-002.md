# Can I change my delivery address after ordering?

Yes, as long as the order is still **Pending** or **Confirmed**. Open the order in My Orders and choose **Edit Address**. If the order has already shipped, contact support; we can try to redirect it, but this may add one business day and a fee may apply if the new address is in a different governorate.
