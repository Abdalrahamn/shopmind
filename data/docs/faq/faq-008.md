# Where are your branches?

ShopMind has five branches: **Nasr City** (City Stars Mall), **Sheikh Zayed** (Arkan Plaza), **Maadi** (Road 9), **Alexandria** (San Stefano Mall) and **Mansoura** (El Gomhoreya Street). All branches open daily from **10 AM to 11 PM**, and Friday from **2 PM to 11 PM**.
