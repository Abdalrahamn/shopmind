# How do I track my order?

Open **My Orders** in your account and select the order to see its current status and the courier tracking link. You also receive an SMS with the tracking link when the order ships. Statuses are: Pending, Confirmed, Shipped, Out for delivery, Delivered and Cancelled. If the status has not changed for more than 3 business days, contact support with your order number.
