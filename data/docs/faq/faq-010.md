# Do you offer trade-in for old phones?

Yes. With **ShopMind Trade-In** you can trade your old smartphone or laptop for a discount on a new device. Bring the device to any branch for a free check; the trade-in value depends on the model, condition and battery health. The value is applied immediately as a discount on your new purchase. Trade-in is available in branches only, not online.
