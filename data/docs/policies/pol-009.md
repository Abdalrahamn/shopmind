# ShopMind Points

## Earning points

- You earn **1 point for every 10 EGP** spent on delivered orders.
- Points are added **14 days after delivery** (after the return window for opened products ends).
- Gold members (spent 100,000 EGP or more in the last 12 months) earn **double points**.

## Using points

- **100 points = 10 EGP** discount at checkout.
- You can pay up to **30%** of an order with points.
- Points expire **12 months** after they are earned.

## Returns

If you return a product, the points earned from it are removed. Points used on a returned order are given back to your account.
