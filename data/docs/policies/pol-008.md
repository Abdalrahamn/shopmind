# Privacy Policy Summary

- We collect your name, phone number, email, addresses and order history to deliver orders and provide support.
- Payment card data is handled by our payment gateway; we do not store full card numbers.
- We never sell your personal data. We share it only with couriers, payment providers and finance partners to complete your order.
- Marketing messages are sent only if you opt in, and you can unsubscribe at any time from your account settings.
- You can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account by contacting support. Order records needed for tax purposes are kept for 5 years as required by law.
- Customer support chats, including chats with our AI assistant, may be reviewed to improve service quality.
