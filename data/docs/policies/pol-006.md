# Order Cancellation Policy

## Cancelling before shipping

You can cancel an order for free while its status is **Pending** or **Confirmed**. Open My Orders and choose **Cancel Order**. Prepaid orders are refunded in full to the original payment method.

## Cancelling after shipping

Once an order is **Shipped**, it can no longer be cancelled from the website. You can refuse the delivery at the door, or accept it and request a return under the Return Policy. Shipping fees are not refunded in this case.

## Cancellations by ShopMind

We may cancel an order if a product is out of stock after you ordered, if the price was clearly wrong, or if the payment could not be verified. We notify you by SMS and email and refund any amount paid within 2 business days.

## Partial cancellation

For orders with several items, you can cancel individual items before the order ships. Free shipping is recalculated: if the remaining total drops below the free shipping threshold, the standard fee is added.
