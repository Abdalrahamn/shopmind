# Payment Methods

We accept the following payment methods:

- **Credit and debit cards:** Visa, Mastercard and Meeza.
- **Cash on delivery (COD):** available for orders up to **15,000 EGP**. A fee of **15 EGP** is added to COD orders.
- **Mobile wallets:** Vodafone Cash, Orange Cash, Etisalat Cash and InstaPay.
- **Instalments:** see the Instalment Plans policy.
- **ShopMind wallet balance and gift cards.**

## Payment security

Card payments are processed by a certified payment gateway. ShopMind never stores your full card number. Every online card payment is verified with 3-D Secure (a one-time code from your bank).

## Failed payments

If your payment fails but money was taken from your account, the amount is usually returned by your bank within **3 to 7 business days**. If it is not returned, contact support with your order number.

## Invoices

An electronic tax invoice is sent by email after delivery and is always available in My Orders.
