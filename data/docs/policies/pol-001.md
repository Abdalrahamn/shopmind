# Return and Refund Policy

At ShopMind Electronics we want you to be happy with every purchase. This policy explains when and how you can return a product and how refunds are paid.

## Return windows

- **Unopened products:** you can return them within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund.
- **Opened products in perfect condition:** you can return them within **14 days** of delivery. The product must include all original accessories, manuals and packaging.
- **Defective products:** if a product stops working within the first **30 days**, we replace it or refund it in full, whether it was opened or not. After 30 days, defects are handled under the warranty policy.

## Products that cannot be returned

- Items sold in the **Clearance** section are final sale and cannot be returned unless they are defective.
- Opened software, gift cards, and digital licence codes.
- In-ear headphones and earbuds once the hygiene seal is broken, unless they are defective.
- Products damaged by misuse, liquid, drops, or unauthorised repair.

## How to start a return

1. Open **My Orders** in your account and select the order.
2. Choose **Request Return**, pick the items and the reason.
3. Our team reviews the request within **2 business days**.
4. Once approved, a courier collects the item from your address for free (Cairo and Giza) or you can drop it at any ShopMind branch.

## Refunds

- Refunds go back to the **original payment method**.
- Card payments: **5 to 10 business days** after we receive and inspect the item.
- Cash on delivery orders: refunded to a ShopMind wallet within **2 business days**, or by bank transfer within **7 business days** if you request it.
- Shipping fees are refunded only when the return is due to our error or a defective product.

## Exchanges

You can exchange a product for a different model or colour within the same return window. If the new product costs more, you pay the difference; if it costs less, we refund the difference.
