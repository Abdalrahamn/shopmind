# Instalment Plans

You can pay for orders of **3,000 EGP or more** in monthly instalments.

## Bank card instalments

- **0% interest for 6 months** with cards from partner banks (CIB, Banque Misr, National Bank of Egypt, QNB).
- **12 months** are available with an interest rate set by your bank.
- The instalment plan is chosen on the payment page; your bank converts the purchase within one billing cycle.

## Consumer finance companies

- valU, Contact, Souhoola and Aman: plans from **6 to 36 months**.
- You need an active account with the finance company. Approval and limits are decided by them, not by ShopMind.
- A down payment may be required depending on your limit.

## Returns on instalment orders

If you return a product bought in instalments, ShopMind cancels the transaction with the bank or finance company. Any administrative fees charged by the bank or finance company are not refundable by ShopMind.
