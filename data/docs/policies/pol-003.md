# Warranty Policy

## Warranty length

- Every product sold by ShopMind has an official warranty. The warranty length is written on each product page and on the invoice.
- Phones, laptops, tablets and TVs: usually **12 to 24 months** of manufacturer warranty.
- Accessories (chargers, cables, cases): **6 months** of ShopMind warranty.
- Products in the Clearance section keep their manufacturer warranty, but ShopMind's extra warranty does not apply.

## What the warranty covers

Manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship under normal use: for example a phone that does not charge, a laptop with a dead keyboard, or headphones with one silent side.

## What the warranty does not cover

- Physical damage: cracked screens, dents, bent ports.
- Liquid damage, even on water-resistant devices.
- Battery wear after 12 months (battery capacity naturally drops over time).
- Software problems caused by unofficial software, rooting or jailbreaking.
- Damage from repairs done outside an authorised service centre.

## How to make a warranty claim

1. Keep your invoice; you can download it any time from **My Orders**.
2. Request a warranty service from the order page or visit any ShopMind branch.
3. We inspect the device within **3 business days** and send it to the authorised service centre if needed.
4. Typical repair time is **7 to 14 business days**. If the same defect happens **three times**, we replace the device.

## ShopMind Care+ (extended warranty)

You can add **ShopMind Care+** at checkout to extend the warranty by **12 months** and add one accidental damage repair (screen or liquid). Care+ costs **8% of the product price** and must be bought with the product or within 7 days of delivery.
