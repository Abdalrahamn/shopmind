# Return and Refund Policy (archived 2024 version)

> This version of the policy is archived and no longer applies to new orders. It is kept for orders placed before 1 September 2026.

## Return windows

- All products, opened or unopened, can be returned within **14 days** of delivery.
- Defective products can be returned within **14 days**; after that the warranty applies.

## Refunds

- Card refunds are processed within **14 business days**.
- Cash on delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer only.
- Return shipping costs **50 EGP** and is paid by the customer in all cases.
