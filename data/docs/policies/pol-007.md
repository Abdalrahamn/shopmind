# Price Match Guarantee

If you find the same product cheaper at an authorised Egyptian retailer within **7 days** of your purchase, we refund the difference.

## Conditions

- The product must be identical: same brand, model, storage, colour and condition (new).
- The competitor must be an authorised dealer with the product in stock and delivery available in Egypt.
- Prices from marketplaces with third-party sellers, auctions, flash sales, coupon codes and bundle offers are excluded.
- The difference is refunded to your ShopMind wallet.

## How to request a price match

Contact support with your order number and a link or photo of the competitor's offer. We answer within 2 business days.
