# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Delivery areas and times

| Area | Standard delivery | Express delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Cairo and Giza | 1 to 2 business days | Same day (order before 2 PM) |
| Alexandria, Delta and Canal cities | 2 to 4 business days | Next business day |
| Upper Egypt, Red Sea and Sinai | 3 to 6 business days | Not available |

Business days are Sunday to Thursday, excluding public holidays.

## Shipping fees

- **Free standard shipping** on orders of **2,000 EGP or more** to Cairo and Giza.
- Standard shipping to Cairo and Giza for smaller orders: **50 EGP**.
- Standard shipping to all other governorates: **75 EGP**, free on orders of **5,000 EGP or more**.
- Express delivery: **120 EGP**, never free.
- Large items (TVs over 55 inches, gaming chairs) have a fixed fee of **150 EGP** outside Cairo and Giza.

## Order tracking

After your order ships you receive an SMS and email with a tracking link. You can also see the status in **My Orders**: Pending, Confirmed, Shipped, Out for delivery, Delivered, or Cancelled.

## Failed delivery

The courier tries to deliver **twice**. If both attempts fail, the order returns to our warehouse and is cancelled. Prepaid orders are refunded minus the shipping fee.

## Inspecting your order

Please check the package in front of the courier. If the box is visibly damaged, you can refuse the delivery and we will send a replacement at no cost.
